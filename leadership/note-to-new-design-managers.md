## Context

Hardik Pandya's guide for new design managers, covering time management, communication, team leadership, and design craft. Practical, actionable advice from someone who has done the role.

**Source:** [Note to New Design Managers](https://hvpandya.com/design-managers)

**Attachment:** [Note to New Design Managers - Hardik Pandya.pdf](https://github.com/user-attachments/files/17651413/Note.to.New.Design.Managers.Hardik.Pandya.pdf)

## Managing your time

Structure your day around top priorities intentionally. Decline meetings lacking clear purpose or your contribution. Review designs first thing and provide actionable feedback quickly. Success depends on two factors: quality of work delivered and speed of delivery. Prioritize based on organizational objectives from leadership, not floor-level hearsay.

## Communication

- Use conclusive, definitive language; check for potential misinterpretation
- Use simple words and bullet points; break down sentences
- Require acknowledgment and written timeframes for assigned work
- Verbal agreements don't count; document everything
- Respond quickly to direct reports and bosses
- Have substantive debates in person synchronously for nuance and tonality
- Escalate to calls after five written exchanges on contentious topics
- Conduct mini roadshows with key stakeholders on major initiatives

## Prioritization and scoping

Always ask: "Why is this most important?" and "What did we explore instead?" Link priorities directly to company objectives. Identify the single most critical element; scope projects around it. You are accountable for how your team spends time.

## Team management

Push scope incrementally until reaching natural limits. Demand excellence while providing clear prioritization and support. Set specific timeframes, even day-level granularity. Protect team bandwidth fiercely. Celebrate achievements publicly. Deliver critical feedback immediately. Set and follow team rituals. Model excellence in small details: punctuality, file organization, communication quality.

## Design leadership

Command respect through design excellence yourself. Take on 1-2 complex projects with direct reports. Seek continuous feedback on your craft. Showcase craft excellence across the team.

## Leadership behavior

Demonstrate optimism; avoid amplifying negativity. Be a confidence source for team and stakeholders. Never respond while angry. Always keep commitments or apologize visibly.
