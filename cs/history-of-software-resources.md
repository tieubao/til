## Context

A curated collection of links covering different angles of software history, from timelines and scholarly papers to niche topics like shareware and accessibility.

## Resources

- [Timeline of Computer History](http://www.computerhistory.org/timeline/?category=sl) - illustrated timeline of major software and hardware achievements, maintained by the Computer History Museum
- [The Early History of Software Engineering](https://www.cs.colorado.edu/~kena/classes/5828/s99/comments/srinivasan/01-29-1999.html) - milestones in software engineering from the "pioneering era" of the 1950s
- [Software Industry Special Interest Group](http://www.softwarehistory.org/) - nonprofit dedicated to preserving early software development history
- [Research on the History of Software Engineering (PDF)](http://citeseer.ist.psu.edu/viewdoc/download;jsessionid=2127D1E5AC3F4260CCA35122C019BBE4?doi=10.1.1.89.2506&rep=rep1&type=pdf) - scholarly papers including retrospectives and interviews
- [The History of Software Accessibility](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Mozilla/Accessibility/Software_accessibility_today) - how software changed to be more accessible
- [History of Shareware](http://www.thunderboltgames.com/feature/the-history-of-shareware) - origin and development of shareware distribution model
- [A Brief History of Word Processing](https://web.stanford.edu/~bkunde/fb-press/articles/wdprhist.html) - timeline of word processing software and office productivity suites
