## Question

What is special about alkaline water, and do the health claims hold up?

## Answer

Alkaline water (pH 8-9+) has become a major marketing phenomenon, but the claims far outstrip the evidence.

**The core flaw in the premise:** Your stomach has a pH of about 1.5-3.5. When you drink alkaline water, stomach acid neutralizes it almost immediately. The idea that drinking slightly alkaline water can meaningfully change your body's pH is fundamentally wrong. Your body already regulates blood pH extremely tightly (7.35-7.45) through your kidneys and lungs. If it didn't, you'd be dead.

![pH reality check showing stomach acid neutralizes alkaline water instantly](https://assets.han-ws.workers.dev/i/2026/04/alkaline-water-ph-reality-check.svg)

**What the research actually shows:**

- No strong clinical evidence that alkaline water prevents cancer, slows aging, or "detoxifies" the body. Those are marketing claims.
- A few small studies suggest it might help with acid reflux (pepsin deactivation at pH 8.8) and possibly improve hydration status in athletes. Preliminary, small-sample studies, not settled science.
- The mineral content (calcium, magnesium, potassium) in some natural alkaline waters can be beneficial, but you get those minerals from food too.

**Where it can cause harm:**

- Lowering stomach acidity impairs digestion and nutrient absorption
- Can interfere with certain medications
- Excessive alkalinity can cause metabolic alkalosis (nausea, vomiting, tremors)

## Key Takeaway

Your body regulates its own pH with extreme precision. Alkaline water gets neutralized by stomach acid instantly and cannot override that system. The premium price is paying for marketing, not science. Regular filtered water does the job.
