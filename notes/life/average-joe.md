## Context

A personal reflection by a self-described "average Joe" on what it means to live with ordinary abilities while holding extraordinary dreams. Originally posted at hongchao.me/about/.

## The average Joe condition

An average Joe is neither physically attractive nor intellectually superior. Procrastination is a constant companion, perseverance fluctuates at the mediocre level, and genuine pride in personal achievements is rare.

The core tension: an average Joe in denial carries the same dreams as exceptional people. When those dreams don't materialize, the reflexive move is to reach for excuses, because the real reasons are harder to admit: lack of discipline, lack of perseverance, and perhaps a failure to work smarter.

## The value of denial

There is nothing wrong with being average. Happiness doesn't require exceptionality. But the interesting question is: how far can an average person push themselves? If an average Joe can do it, most people can too.

The author's stance is to stay in the phase of denial a little longer, to keep learning, keep taking notes, and see what happens when an ordinary person refuses to stop trying.
