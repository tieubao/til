## Context

A list of life principles attributed to Pavel Durov, founder of Telegram and VK. Covers health, learning, goal-setting, and time management.

## The principles

1. **Become a pro in what you like.** Mastery in something you enjoy makes you happier than being mediocre at something lucrative.

2. **Clean up what you consume.** Drop junk food, drinks, and smoking. No magic diets; stick to organic vegetables, fruits, and water.

3. **Know your limits.** Being vegetarian or refusing alcohol is not the key. Self-awareness about your boundaries is.

4. **Learn foreign languages.** Expands your perception of society, opens career opportunities and personal development.

5. **Read constantly.** Self-improvement books and professional material. Audiobooks work if you're too lazy to read.

6. **Make days off useful.** Family, cinema, picnics, buy lunch for the needy. Expand the scope of contacts with the world.

7. **Write, no matter what.** Even if only your diary. The point is learning to talk to yourself.

8. **Set concrete goals.** Write them down. Ideas must be concrete, measurable, and time-based.

9. **Learn to touch-type.** Think about what you're writing, not where the letters are. Decisions quick, actions immediate; don't put things off.

10. **Minimize passive consumption.** Less TV, aimless internet browsing, games, and social networks.
