## Context

A one-question burnout triage framework from Ben McCormick. Useful for checking in on yourself or teammates before burnout becomes critical.

## The question

> If you take the pace and quality of the last 2 months of your life and repeated it again and again, how long would you be able to sustain it?

Why 2 months: long enough to be representative (cuts out one-off deadline rushes), short enough to check in regularly, and most people can clearly recall their last 2 months.

## Three responses

**"I can't go on like this."** Already well into burnout. Tasks are being dropped, personal needs unmet, everyday life is overwhelming.

**"I can make this work, but..."** The most interesting response. Visualizing a repeating future makes people hone in on what they're missing: time with kids, investing in themselves, leaving work at the office, living in the moment.

**"I love my life, and I can keep doing this forever."** Treasure it. Check in regularly to make sure it stays true.

## What next

Principles for handling partial or complete burnout:
- Optimize for the long term
- Tie your time to your values
- Living in an unsustainable place for a season can make sense, but weigh the impact on yourself, family, and teammates against the long-term value

Key distinction: "it's only a season if it has a defined endpoint or offramp." If you don't know when it ends, it's not a season. It's your life.
