## Context

As startups grow past the early stage, many face a gap between the technical co-founder doing all the coding and the need for strategic technology leadership. The interim CTO role fills this gap.

**Source:** [The Rise of the Interim CTO](https://launchany.com/the-rise-of-the-interim-cto/)

## What an interim CTO does

A temporary executive who provides technology leadership to early-stage and growing startups. Responsibilities include defining software architecture, establishing development processes, overseeing development teams, evaluating technologies, and influencing company strategy.

## When you need one

- The startup lacks technical expertise and knowledge
- Transitioning from undisciplined development to structured, scalable processes
- Moving from product/market fit discovery to growth acceleration
- The technical co-founder is overwhelmed by immediate coding rather than long-term vision

## The key distinction

Technical co-founders often focus on immediate coding tasks. Interim CTOs maintain "the 30,000-foot level view of technology" while still getting hands-on to make things happen. They typically transition companies toward hiring a full-time CTO.

## Benefits

- Save startups $20k-100k or more by avoiding costly mistakes
- Balance high-level technology vision with hands-on execution
- Experienced at recruiting and mentoring development staff
- Usually better connected with developers for recruiting

## What to look for

Strong candidates have prior startup founding experience, deep technical knowledge across various domains, cloud infrastructure expertise, and proven leadership capabilities.
