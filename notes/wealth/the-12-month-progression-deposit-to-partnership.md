# The 12-Month Progression: From Deposit to Partnership

![Pre-game protocol and 12-month progression](https://assets.han-ws.workers.dev/i/2026/04/wealth-03-pregame-and-progression.svg)

There is a predictable timeline for how trust builds with older/wealthier people. Most people quit in month 2-3 because nothing visible is happening.

## Phase 1: Deposit (Month 1-3)

**Trust: ~15%. Deal flow: 0%.**

You are being sorted. Show up, listen (70/30 ratio), bring small signals, follow up next day referencing something specific. Share useful information same-day. Keep every small promise.

**What you get**: nothing visible. **What is happening**: invisible tests are running. Target category: "thinking independently, worth watching."

## Phase 2: Recognition (Month 4-6)

**Trust: ~40%. Deal flow: ~10%.**

They ask your opinion on something real. They give you a small task with ambiguous instructions. They share mildly confidential information.

**The disagreement moment**: They say something you disagree with. Nod along = stay in "nice kid" category forever. Nuanced counter-opinion = pass the independence test.

## Phase 3: Integration (Month 7-9)

**Trust: ~65%. Deal flow: ~35%.**

They mention a deal in a way that is clearly not casual. First real deal flow. Your move: don't jump at it. Ask a judgment question. Desperation undoes everything.

**What you get**: curated introductions, each an endorsement of your character.

## Phase 4: Partnership (Month 10-12+)

**Trust: ~85%. Deal flow: ~60%.**

They start asking what you think. The lens flips. You have something they need: a different vantage point.

**The final test**: can you handle this without becoming entitled?

## Why most people never get past month 3

1. **Invisible progress**: nothing comes back, they start pitching or stop showing up
2. **Failed confidentiality test**: shared info gets repeated, door closes silently
3. **Pleasant and forgettable**: show up, listen, are nice, bring zero signal

## Pre-game protocol (10 minutes before any interaction)

1. Research their recent moves (one deal, one decision)
2. Prepare one signal to bring (a trend, a data point)
3. Prepare one judgment question ("What would you watch for if...")
4. Phone to silent, in bag
5. Know your drinking constraint before arrival
6. Kill every ask in your head
