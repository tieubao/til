## Overview

On February 28, 2026, the US and Israel launched joint military strikes on Iran, including the assassination of Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei. Iran retaliated by closing the Strait of Hormuz to most shipping traffic. The strait handles roughly 20% of global seaborne oil trade (~20 million barrels/day). The IEA has characterized this as the "largest supply disruption in the history of the global oil market."

## Transmission mechanism

Australia's vulnerability is indirect but severe. Australia imports little crude directly from the Middle East. However, 70-80% of its refined petrol and diesel comes via Asian refineries (Singapore, South Korea, China) that depend on Middle Eastern crude flowing through the strait.
