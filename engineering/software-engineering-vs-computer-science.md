## Context

A professor answers a common student question: why study software engineering instead of computer science? The two fields focus on different aspects of computing and lead to different career paths.

**Source:** [Software Engineering and Computer Science](http://science-technology.vn/?p=2588)

## The core difference

**Computer Science** focuses on the science of algorithm development - efficient code to run on computers. This is why CS programs require many years of mathematics and several programming languages.

**Software Engineering** focuses on the engineering of process management - efficient software development to solve complex issues. A key principle: for every 10% increase in problem complexity, there is a 100% increase in software solution complexity. You need to engineer the process rather than relying on algorithms alone.

## How they differ in practice

| Aspect | Computer Science | Software Engineering |
|--------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Focus | Efficient algorithms, scientific methods | Process management, development lifecycle |
| Quality definition | Mostly about software defects | Collection of attributes: performance, usability, portability, reliability, testability, modifiability |
| Defect approach | Detection and removal (testing) | Prevention at each phase (inspections, reviews) |
| Teamwork | Less emphasized | Core to the curriculum |
| Time allocation | ~40% on testing | More time on reviews and inspections at each phase |
| Career trajectory | Technical side: algorithms, formal methods | Management side: project management, system architecture, process improvement |

## Key insights

- A highly skilled developer can be up to 30 times more productive than a mediocre one. The most important factor in solving complex problems is not tools, algorithms, or techniques, but the skills of the developers themselves.
- Quality is not the same as satisfying users or meeting requirements. User satisfaction = quality product + meets requirements + delivered when needed + appropriate cost.
- Software engineers believe the quality of the product depends on the quality of the process that creates it. Focus on each phase to remove defects early rather than spending all the time on testing at the end.
