## Context

Ethereum's token standard ecosystem evolved from simple fungible tokens (ERC-20) to specialized standards for different use cases. The emergence of security tokens (STOs) represented a shift from unregulated ICOs toward SEC-compliant tokenized securities.

**Source:** [Ethereum Token Standards - ChronoBank](https://blog.chronobank.io/ethereum-token-standards-19fbcc54fe27), [Security Token Ecosystem Guide - Tatiana Koffman](https://medium.com/@tatianakoffman/your-official-guide-to-the-security-token-ecosystem-61a805673db7)

## What are security tokens?

Security tokens represent financial securities compliant with SEC regulations. Unlike utility tokens, they provide concrete financial rights: equity, dividends, voting privileges, and buyback options. Rights are embedded directly into smart contracts and traded on blockchain exchanges.

## Key advantages of tokenization

- **Access to global capital pools** - Issuers can reach international investors
- **Asset fractionalization** - Enables smaller investment minimums
- **Improved liquidity** - Compared to traditional private securities
- **Lower issuance costs** - Reduced intermediary overhead
- **Programmable compliance** - KYC/AML baked into token mechanics

## The STO process

A typical STO takes about six months:
1. Legal structuring and marketing (2 months)
2. Technical platform integration (1 month)
3. Investor roadshows targeting institutional and crypto participants (3 months)

## Ecosystem layers

**Issuance platforms** - Polymath, Harbor, Securitize handle token creation and regulatory compliance. Harbor's R-Token integrates regulatory requirements directly into token mechanics, automating accreditation verification.

**Hybrid solutions** - Templum combines initial offerings with secondary trading, functioning as a registered alternative trading system and broker-dealer.

**Exchanges** - tZERO and OpenFinance Network provide trading infrastructure. tZERO offers 24-hour trading with broker network integration.

**Liquidity solutions** - Bancor and AirSwap enable cross-market trading, addressing the long tail of low-demand, low-volume tokenized products.

## Early movers

- **Blockchain Capital (BCap)** - Pioneered the template with a $10M offering
- **SPiCE VC** - Tokenized venture capital fund interests, providing immediate liquidity vs traditional 7-12 year fund lockups
- **PropertyCoin, Slice** - Real estate tokenization
- **Lottery.com** - Lottery industry tokenization
